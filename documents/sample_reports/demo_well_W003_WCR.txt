OIL INDIA LIMITED (PROTOTYPE DEMO ENVIRONMENT - SYNTHETIC DATA)
FINAL WELL COMPLETION REPORT (WCR) - SECTION 6: WELL CONTROL & HAZARDS
================================================================================
Well Name: Nahorkatiya-88
Field: Nahorkatiya Field
Formation: Kopili Formation
Depth Interval: 4120.0 m - 4155.0 m

INCIDENT REPORT & OBSERVATIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Event Type: KICK (Gas Influx / Overpressure)
Severity: CRITICAL
Depth: 4135.0 m

Cause:
Drilled rapidly into an unanticipated abnormal overpressured fractured gas sand streak within the upper Kopili Formation. Drilling break observed (ROP jumped from 6.5 m/hr to 18.0 m/hr). Formation pore pressure gradient was 1.44 SG equivalent vs active mud density of 1.34 SG, inducing instantaneous gas influx.

Impact:
Active pit volume surged by 22 bbls in 6 minutes. Gas units on mud logging unit spiked from 45 units to 1850 units. Shut-In Drillpipe Pressure (SIDPP) reached 360 psi; Shut-In Casing Pressure (SICP) reached 480 psi.

Mitigation Actions Taken:
1. Driller executed soft shut-in procedure: spaced out tool joint, shut down mud pumps, opened HCR choke line, closed annular blowout preventer (BOP).
2. Monitored and recorded stabilized SIDPP (360 psi) and SICP (480 psi).
3. Prepared kill sheet and mixed 1.48 SG weighted polymer kill mud.
4. Successfully circulated out gas kick using Wait & Weight (Engineer's) Method via automated choke console while maintaining constant bottom hole pressure.
5. Replaced complete hole volume with 1.48 SG kill mud; flow check confirmed well statically dead.

Lessons Learned & Recommendations:
- Perform flow check immediately upon noticing any ROP drilling break > 4 m/hr in Kopili and lower Barail formations.
- Ensure degasser, choke manifold transducers, and gas alarms are tested and certified prior to entering Kopili top.
