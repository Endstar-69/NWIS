INCIDENT REPORT & OBSERVATIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Event Type: MUD_LOSS (Lost Circulation)
Severity: HIGH
Depth Interval: 3418.0 m - 3432.0 m

Cause:
Encountered highly permeable, partially depleted sandstone reservoir stringer in Barail Sandstone. Hydrostatic pressure of 1.30 SG mud column exceeded depleted reservoir pore pressure (1.12 SG equivalent), causing micro-fracturing and matrix seepage.

Impact:
Active pit volume decreased rapidly by 52 bbls over 25 minutes. Standpipe pressure dropped from 2450 psi to 2280 psi (-170 psi drop). Return flow paddle indicator dropped to 65%.

Mitigation Actions Taken:
1. Immediately picked up drillstring off bottom and stopped rotary.
2. Reduced main mud pump flow rate from 2100 LPM to 1750 LPM.
3. Mixed and spotted 40 bbl high-fluid-loss LCM pill containing 25 ppb medium Calcium Carbonate (CaCO3) and 15 ppb coarse walnut shells.
4. Squeezed 15 bbl into matrix under 180 psi hesitation pressure. Allowed pill to soak for 2 hours.
5. Reconditioned active mud system density down to 1.25 SG. Full circulation successfully restored with zero pit loss.

Lessons Learned & Recommendations:
- Pre-treat active polymer mud system with 15-20 ppb fine CaCO3 bridging agent prior to drilling within 30m of Barail Sandstone upper horizon.
- Continuously monitor delta flow paddle trends during drilling connections in offset wells.
